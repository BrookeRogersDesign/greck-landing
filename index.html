About

MacKenzie Greckel is a painter. Her canvas just happens to be a bag.

Every bag is hand sewn and hand painted in acrylic and marker, made entirely by her. The work runs in three series, and no two pieces are alike. Until now you had to catch them on Depop. Soon they'll have a home of their own.
